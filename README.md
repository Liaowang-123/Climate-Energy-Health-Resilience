# Wellcome Project 2025–2027

## Overview
This repository contains research outputs, datasets, computational models, dashboards, surveys, publications, and policy materials related to the Wellcome Project on health co-benefits of clean energy transition.

## Repository Structure

- data/ — datasets and metadata
- models/ — computational models and notebooks
- dashboards/ — visualization tools and dashboards
- surveys/ — questionnaires and survey materials
- publications/ — papers and reports
- policy-whitepapers/ — policy recommendations and white papers
- case-studies/ — community and environmental case studies

## Research Areas
- Energy justice
- Climate health
- Weatherization
- Community microgrids
- Environmental health
- Policy analysis
