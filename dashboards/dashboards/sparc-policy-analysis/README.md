# 🏛 SPARC Policy Analysis Dashboard

## AI-powered Policy Analysis for Community Resilience

The **SPARC Policy Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive platform for exploring South Carolina resilience and planning documents using artificial intelligence, text analytics, and geospatial visualization.

It supports research on community resilience, hazard mitigation, housing, and policy priorities across counties and time.

# Overview

The SPARC Dashboard enables users to explore a large corpus of resilience-related planning documents across South Carolina.

The platform combines:

- AI-assisted document extraction
- policy domain classification
- county-level exploration
- hazard analysis
- temporal trend visualization
- interactive summaries of the document corpus

It is designed to support interdisciplinary research, policy analysis, and community resilience planning.

The dashboard is intended for exploratory research and synthesis of planning documents.

Its outputs can be used to:

- identify recurring policy themes
- compare hazard emphasis across counties
- examine changes in planning activity over time
- support case studies and policy recommendations
