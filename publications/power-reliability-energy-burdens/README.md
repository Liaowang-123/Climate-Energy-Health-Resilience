# Power Reliability, Energy Burdens, and Climate Change Beliefs

This folder contains publication materials related to electric power reliability, energy burdens, and climate change beliefs in the United States.

Authors:
Hang Shuai, Chien-Fei Chen, Benjamin Sovacool, Suzanna Sumkhuu, and Zhenglai Shen

Description:
This study examines the relationship between power outages, household energy burdens, and public climate change beliefs using county-level geospatial regression analysis across the United States.

Keywords:
Power outages, energy burdens, climate change beliefs, geospatial regression, energy justice
